### Description
- He enters from the left, walks all the way across the header, exits completely on the right, waits briefly, then returns from the left, endlessly.
- His position is time-based, not page-load-based: when a visitor navigates to another page or reloads, Santa continues walking from where he left off instead of restarting at the edge.
- He can extend above or below the header, but is neatly clipped at the left and right edges.
- The hover effect from version 1.0.0 (Santa lifts up with a shadow) is retained, with smoother animation.

### This fork adds
- Adjustments for the Style IMC-Prosilver.
- A snowdrift that Santa Claus walks over.
- A snow flurry (Copyright by Fabian Schlieper). Revised and adapted by IMC-GER.
- Snow flurry changes:
  - Revised variable names.
  - When you switch pages, the  Snowflake data will be saved and carried over to the new page.
  - The function for generating snowflakes has been optimized.
